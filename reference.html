Summary:

While trying to write to his friend studying abroad in Moscow, Shane Hollander accidentally reaches Ilya Rozanov.

Phone: click on the letters (if the text still looks off/overlaps, try switching to desktop mode on your browser!)

Dear Tim,

Hey! How are you doing?

As per your request before you left, here I am, writing to you. Like I told you, I’m not really used to sending letters (unless it’s to my extended family, and even then, maybe once a year). But I guess I can see the appeal. It does feel more thoughtful than a text. Even if, honestly, waiting days and weeks for a reply is kind of nerve-wracking, haha.

Anyway, how’s life at MSU? I hope your semester’s going well so far. Your replacement on the team is a bit rough around the edges, but we’ll figure it out. How’s the hockey team over there? I’d be curious to know how different their play style is from ours. Are they giving you minimal ice time, since you’re not staying long term? Though knowing how you play, any decent team would be stupid not to use you.

Also, turns out I didn’t manage to find a new roommate. Everyone I talked to either ghosted me after a few messages, or I had to veto them. One guy straight-up told me he “needed” to bring someone home every night. No, thank you.


It’s fine for now, even if things are a bit tight financially. But all that to say… if you didn’t already have plans for when you come back, the room’s yours if you want it. If you could let me know as soon as possible (maybe by message, not letter…), that’d be great.

I think that’s all for now. I’ll let this get to you, and hopefully we talk soon.

Take care,
Shane Hollander

First of all, I am sorry. I tried to translate your last name, but I was not sure that I got it right. Rosanov, maybe?

I’m deeply sorry if what I wrote gave you the wrong impression.

I simply did not want to leave you hanging with hopes that I could not fulfill (beside, if you truly do want to come to uOttawa, you should do researches before deciding anything. I wish you the best of luck with your plans to move abroad.



Best wishes,
